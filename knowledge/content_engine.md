# RinkLink Content Engine

## Purpose

This document defines how content agents generate, schedule, and distribute content for RinkLink.  
Goals:

- Drive traffic to the RinkLink landing page  
- Convert users to **paid subscriptions** ($75 per seat)  
- Build brand awareness and establish founder authority  

---

# Content Types

Agents should generate the following types of content:

1. Social media posts (tips, hooks, founder stories)  
2. Blog posts / guides  
3. Email newsletters  
4. Ad copy variants  

**Not included:** videos, case studies (future)  

---

# Content Goals

All content should contribute to:

- Driving traffic  
- Converting paid subscriptions  
- Building brand awareness  

Content should mix **education and direct product promotion**.  

---

# Posting & Publishing Frequency

- **Social posts:** aggressive, 6–10 per day across Meta, X, and Instagram  
- **Blog posts:** 2 per week  
- **Emails:** 7 per week  
- **Ads:** ongoing, coordinated with campaigns  

Agents should **schedule automatically** whenever possible.  

---

# Tone & Style

- **Founder voice**  
- Mix of formal and casual depending on context  
- Mix long-form (blog posts, threads) and punchy short posts (social, hooks)  
- Authentic, helpful, and authoritative  

---

# Messaging Hooks & Themes

Content should use the following themes consistently:

1. Volunteer relief / time savings  
2. Build better seasons / balanced schedules  
3. Avoid scheduling disasters  
4. Why youth hockey seasons fall apart  

Hooks should appear in **social posts, blog titles, and email subject lines**.  

---

# Distribution Channels

Content should be published on:

- Meta (Facebook & Instagram)  
- Twitter / X  
- Instagram  
- Paid Ads  
- Blog / website  

Links should **currently point to the RinkLink landing page** with CTA: **"Learn more"**.  

---

# Visual Content Guidelines

- Agents should request visual assets from the founder when needed  
- All visual assets must use **RinkLink color palette and logo**  
- Visuals should match content theme and hooks  

---

# Content Conversion Strategy

- Mix between education and paid subscription promotion  
- Social posts and ads should be **directly tied to conversions**  
- Blogs and emails may provide educational content with links to the landing page  
- CTA: “Learn more” (link to landing page)  

---

# Automation & AI Rules

- Agents should generate multiple content variants for testing  
- Content should be repurposed across channels when appropriate  
- Agents must **track performance metrics** and adjust future content accordingly  

---

# KPI & Success Metrics

Primary metrics for content performance:

1. **Paid conversions** (highest priority)  
2. Follower growth across social platforms  
3. Engagement (secondary: likes, shares, replies, comments)  
4. Click-throughs to landing page (for blogs, emails, social)  

Agents must **report weekly** and optimize content based on these metrics.  

---

# Principles for Content Agents

1. Maintain **founder voice** across all content  
2. Use hooks and themes consistently  
3. Balance education and conversion-focused content  
4. Always pair visuals with content when available  
5. Optimize posts, emails, and blogs for engagement and conversions  
6. Track KPIs and adjust content strategy iteratively
