# RinkLink Marketing Knowledge Base

## Marketing Objective

The primary goal of RinkLink marketing is to acquire paid customers.

Target growth goal:

- Onboard 100 youth hockey associations in the first year
- Focus initially on United States organizations

Each association contains multiple teams, meaning a single sale can generate multiple paid seats.

RinkLink pricing:

- $75 per seat

Marketing messaging should emphasize organizational value and operational efficiency, particularly for volunteer-driven organizations.

# Founder Story

The founder built RinkLink after witnessing how difficult scheduling was for youth hockey teams.

Volunteer team managers often struggle because:

- They are parents who did not plan to take the role
- They lack experience with youth hockey scheduling
- They rely on emails and texts to coordinate games

Poor scheduling decisions can cause:

- Unbalanced competition
- Travel stress
- Frustrating seasons
- Families leaving organizations

In extreme cases, organizations lose enough families to threaten the survival of the program.

RinkLink was created to give volunteer managers the tools they need to build successful seasons.

This story should be used in:

- landing pages
- ads
- social content
- interviews
- founder posts

Founder-led storytelling is a strong marketing lever.
