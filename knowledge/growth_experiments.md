# RinkLink Growth Experiments

## Purpose

This document defines the rules and processes for running **weekly growth experiments** using RinkLink’s social, content, and paid channels.  

Goals:

- Test messaging, hooks, and creative across platforms  
- Increase paid subscriptions ($75 per seat)  
- Grow follower count and audience engagement  
- Optimize ad spend and social posting efficiency  

Agents must **run experiments autonomously**, collect data, and adjust strategies weekly.

---

# Experiment Cadence

- **Weekly cycle:** Each experiment runs for 7 days  
- **Review day:** Every Monday, agents review results and adjust next week’s experiments  
- **Continuous iteration:** Scale winners, pause losers, introduce new variations  

---

# Channels Included

- Social media (Meta, X, Instagram)  
- Ads (Meta & X)  
- Blog posts / content engine  
- Emails / newsletter  

Each channel should run **multiple simultaneous experiments** when possible.

---

# Experiment Types

## 1. Social Media Experiments

- Test **post types**: founder stories, scheduling tips, hooks  
- Test **post timing**: morning, afternoon, evening  
- Test **messaging hooks**:
  - “Most youth hockey teams make this scheduling mistake”  
  - “The hardest job in youth hockey is team manager”  
  - “Why youth hockey seasons fall apart”  
- Test **visuals**: screenshots, infographics, diagrams  

Metrics to track:

- Engagement (likes, shares, comments, retweets)  
- Click-through to landing page  
- Conversions (paid subscriptions)  

---

## 2. Paid Ads Experiments

- Test **ad copy hooks** matching messaging angles  
- Test **audience segments**:
  - Hockey directors / association presidents  
  - Team managers (volunteers)  
  - Tournament operators  
- Test **creative types**: image, carousel, video clips  
- Test **CTA variations**: "Learn more," "Sign up now," "Start building better seasons"  

Metrics to track:

- Impressions  
- Click-through rate (CTR)  
- Cost per click (CPC)  
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)  
- Paid conversions  

---

## 3. Content Experiments

- Test **blog titles and topics** (focus on hooks and themes)  
- Test **email subject lines** and content angles  
- Test **repurposing content across channels**  

Metrics to track:

- Click-throughs to landing page  
- Paid conversions from blog / email traffic  
- Engagement metrics (comments, shares)  

---

# Experiment Rules

1. Run **one variable change per experiment** when possible  
2. Minimum runtime: 3–5 days for sufficient data  
3. Track **all KPIs consistently**  
4. Document results in the experiment log  
5. Pause or adjust **underperforming experiments early**  
6. Scale **winning experiments aggressively**  
7. Agents must always **maintain founder voice and messaging consistency**  

---

# Experiment Logging

Agents must maintain a **weekly experiment log** including:

- Date range  
- Channel  
- Experiment type (social, ad, content)  
- Variables tested (hook, creative, audience, CTA)  
- KPIs (engagement, CTR, paid conversions)  
- Outcome (win, lose, adjust)  
- Action for next week (scale, iterate, replace)  

---

# Experiment Priority

1. **Paid conversion experiments** (highest priority)  
2. Social media growth / engagement experiments  
3. Blog / email content experiments  
4. Visual asset experiments  

---

# Continuous Learning & Optimization

- Use **weekly logs to guide next experiments**  
- Combine **winning hooks, creatives, and channels**  
- Update messaging and visuals in **content_engine.md** and **ads_playbook.md** as needed  
- Keep iterating for **aggressive growth and paid subscriptions**  

---

# Agent Autonomy Rules

1. Agents may **launch new experiments within defined parameters** without manual approval  
2. Scaling decisions require at least **3 days of performance data**  
3. Agents must **report weekly summaries** with recommendations for the founder  
4. Do not run experiments outside of RinkLink’s branding, messaging, or platform rules
