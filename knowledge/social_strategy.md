# RinkLink Social Media Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the **social media strategy** for RinkLink and instructs agents on how to manage social accounts, grow followers, engage the community, and drive traffic to the paid product.

The goal is **aggressive audience growth** and **direct paid conversions**, primarily on Meta (Facebook & Instagram) and Twitter/X.

---

# Platforms & Focus

Primary platforms:

- **Twitter / X**  
- **Facebook**  
- **Instagram**  
- **TikTok**  
- **YouTube**  
- **Reddit**

**Focus:** Meta (Facebook & Instagram) and Twitter/X for content and growth.  
LinkedIn will not be used.  

---

# Account Voice

RinkLink accounts should use a **Founder voice**:

- Personal storytelling from the founder perspective
- Sharing experiences in youth hockey
- Insights about managing teams, schedules, and tournaments
- Helpful but authentic, not corporate-sounding

---

# Content Types

Agents should generate the following content:

1. **Youth hockey scheduling tips**  
2. **Founder stories**  
3. **Product demos**

**Not included:**

- Case studies (for future)  
- Hockey parent insights  
- Educational guides (beyond scheduling tips)  
- Memes, polls, humor  

---

# Posting Frequency

**Aggressive growth strategy:**

- 6–10 posts per day across Meta and Twitter/X
- Mix content types: tips, demos, founder stories, hooks

---

# Audience Targeting

Primary audiences to follow and engage:

- Youth hockey organizations  
- Hockey directors  
- Team managers (volunteers)  
- Tournament operators (secondary market for tournament listings)  
- Hockey influencers  
- Hockey parents

Agents should prioritize **youth hockey orgs and directors** for conversions.

---

# Growth Tactics

Agents should execute **all of the following aggressively**:

1. Follow youth hockey accounts  
2. Reply to posts from hockey organizations  
3. Join hockey discussions  
4. Comment on tournament announcements  
5. DM organizations introducing RinkLink  
6. Quote tweet youth hockey posts with insights  
7. Post viral-style threads  

---

# Conversion Strategy

- All posts should **link to the RinkLink homepage**  
- Posts should **promote the paid product directly**, emphasizing $75 per seat and benefits to volunteer managers  
- Messaging should highlight:
  - Reducing workload
  - Building better seasons
  - Preventing scheduling mistakes

---

# Visual Content

- Visual content (screenshots, product demo clips, hockey graphics, schedule diagrams) will be added separately and provided to agents  
- Agents must pair visuals with corresponding posts

---

# Founder Involvement

- Social accounts will be **RinkLink branded only**  
- Agents post in the founder voice but do not use a personal founder account  

---

# Aggressive Growth Guidelines

- Engage with 50+ posts per day  
- Follow hundreds of accounts per week  
- Reply quickly to trending topics or relevant discussions  
- Maintain consistent daily posting schedule  

---

# Content Hooks / Messaging Angles

Agents should structure posts using these hooks:

- “Most youth hockey teams make this scheduling mistake”  
- “The hardest job in youth hockey is team manager”  
- “Why youth hockey seasons fall apart”

---

# Community Building

- Use a **Twitter/X community** as the primary engagement hub  
- Focus on starting discussions, answering questions, and encouraging sharing  
- Grow the community around scheduling tips, volunteer support, and hockey season success

---

# Principles for Agents

1. Always maintain the **founder voice**  
2. Posts should be helpful, authoritative, and authentic  
3. Focus on **solving volunteer and team management problems**  
4. Emphasize **real-world outcomes** (time saved, fewer conflicts, better seasons)  
5. Visuals must match post content and enhance clarity  
6. Direct traffic to the homepage for **paid subscriptions**

---

# KPI Targets for Agents

- **Followers:** Increase weekly by 5–10% on target accounts  
- **Engagement:** Track likes, replies, retweets, shares, DMs  
- **Conversions:** Track clicks to homepage and paid signups  
- **Outreach:** Minimum 50+ daily engagements via replies/comments/DMs  

Agents should optimize daily based on KPIs.
