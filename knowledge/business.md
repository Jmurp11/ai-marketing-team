# RinkLink Business Knowledge Base

## Company Overview

RinkLink is a web application designed to help youth hockey organizations manage team scheduling more effectively.

The platform reduces the time coaches and team managers spend coordinating games, evaluating tournaments, and resolving scheduling conflicts. RinkLink introduces AI-assisted tools while ensuring that humans maintain full control over all actions.

The core philosophy of the platform is **assistive AI, not autonomous automation**.

AI suggestions are always reviewed and approved by the user before any action occurs.

---

# Mission

Help youth hockey organizations run smoother seasons by reducing scheduling complexity and administrative overhead.

RinkLink allows coaches and managers to spend less time coordinating logistics and more time focusing on player development.

---

# Target Customers

Primary Users:

- Youth hockey organizations
- Hockey directors
- Club administrators
- Coaches
- Team managers

Secondary Stakeholders:

- Organization board members
- Parents and families indirectly benefiting from better scheduling

---

# Core Problems RinkLink Solves

Youth hockey scheduling today is highly manual and fragmented.

Common issues include:

## Opponent Scheduling

Teams currently rely on:

- Email chains
- Text messages
- Spreadsheet tracking
- Personal networks

Finding balanced opponents often requires dozens of outreach emails.

---

## Schedule Conflicts

Teams frequently create schedules that include:

- Overlapping games
- Unrealistic travel times
- Back-to-back games with insufficient rest
- Same-day long-distance travel

These issues are usually discovered too late.

---

## Tournament Selection

Choosing tournaments is difficult because teams lack visibility into:

- Competition strength
- Existing schedule load
- Travel demands
- Development fit

---

## Organizational Visibility

Most organizations do not have a central system to see:

- All team schedules
- Conflicts across teams
- Travel density across the organization

This makes oversight difficult for hockey directors.

---

# Product Features

## 1. Opponent Discovery

RinkLink helps teams find appropriate opponents.

Capabilities include:

- Discover nearby teams
- Filter by skill level
- Filter by distance
- Rank opponent quality using AI

The system generates **editable outreach emails** that managers can review before sending.

Important rule:

RinkLink **never sends emails automatically**.

---

## 2. Schedule Risk Monitoring

RinkLink continuously evaluates schedules for potential problems.

Examples of flagged risks:

- Overlapping games
- Tight turnaround between games
- Same-day travel risk
- Excessive travel sequences

Alerts are **informational only**.

They do not block scheduling actions.

Users always maintain control.

---

## 3. Tournament Evaluation

Teams can browse tournaments normally.

RinkLink adds AI-assisted evaluation by analyzing:

- Team rating
- Current schedule density
- Travel considerations

The system explains:

- Why a tournament is a good fit
- Why it may not be a good fit

Users can add tournaments directly to their schedule.

---

## 4. AI Assistant

RinkLink includes a conversational assistant that understands team schedules.

The assistant can:

- Answer schedule questions
- Suggest opponents
- Add games
- Add tournaments
- Draft outreach emails
- Help plan team activities

Important rule:

Every action requires **explicit user approval**.

Nothing happens automatically.

---

## 5. Organization Management

RinkLink is designed for **multi-team organizations**, not just individual teams.

Administrative capabilities include:

- Manage organization members
- Control team access permissions
- View schedules across all teams
- Monitor organization scheduling patterns

This allows hockey directors to maintain oversight.

# Key Value Propositions

RinkLink delivers measurable benefits:

- Saves coaches and managers hours each week
- Reduces scheduling conflicts
- Improves competitive balance
- Improves travel planning
- Provides organization-wide visibility
- Works alongside existing workflows

The product does not force organizations to change their existing processes.

---

# Product Philosophy

RinkLink follows a **Human-in-the-Loop AI design philosophy**.

Principles:

1. AI assists but does not act autonomously
2. Users always review actions before they occur
3. Transparency is required for all AI suggestions
4. Users remain in full control

Examples:

- Emails must be reviewed before sending
- Schedule changes require approval
- AI suggestions include explanations

# Constraints and Non-Goals

RinkLink intentionally avoids:

- Automatic game scheduling
- Automatic email sending
- Fully autonomous AI actions
